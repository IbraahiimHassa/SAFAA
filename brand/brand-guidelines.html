02 — The name

Safa — صفا

In Arabic, safa means purity and clarity. It is also Mount Safa in Mecca — the starting point of the sa'i, the search that ends at the spring of Zamzam.

Purity, and a source you can trace back to: the name carries the whole brand in four letters and two languages. We own the meaning — traced to the source — and let the proof speak.

03 — Positioning

Where we win.

Our ground is reverent, single-origin purity — sold as luxury and backed by public proof. Sidr honey anchors the range in something genuinely rare and genuinely ours to tell.

07 — Tone of voice

Calm authority. No hype.

A trusted friend who knows the science: reverent about origin, honest about results, never shouty. We state what a product is and where it's from, and let the customer draw the benefit.

CalmConfidentReverentClearWarm
How we write
  • "Hydrolysed marine collagen peptides, Type I, with vitamin C."
  • "Vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation."
  • "Raw, unfiltered, single-origin Yemeni Sidr honey."
  • "Third-party tested. Results published per batch."
What we avoid
  • Benefit claims a product hasn't earned the right to make.
  • "Boosts / heals / detoxes / reduces wrinkles."
  • "The only / the first / no one else offers it."
  • A halal seal or "certified" before certification is on file.

09 — How we make claims

The standard we hold.

Restraint is the brand. In the EU, it's also the law — and it's what keeps SAFA believed.

i
Authorised wording onlyHealth claims quoted exactly from the EFSA register, attributed to the nutrient that owns the claim (Reg. 1924/2006).
ii
Full, honest labellingAllergens declared (marine collagen ⇒ contains fish), complete nutrition declaration, Dutch on-label for NL (Reg. 1169/2011).
iii
Halal, only when certifiedThe seal and the word appear once a named body, certificate number and expiry are on file and current.
iv
Real proof onlyRatings, reviews and lab figures are genuine and published. Nothing is stated before the report is in hand.
v
No unprovable superlatives, no medical languageOrigin and data instead of "only / first." Medical, treatment, pregnancy and medication questions always go to a human.
