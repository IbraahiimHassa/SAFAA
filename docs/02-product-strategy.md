# SAFA — Product & Reformatting Strategy

The rule, learned from Reformed: **never replace the classic — surround it.** The classic form
stays the hero; new formats are companions, add-ons, and rituals that multiply price points on
the same certified ingredient. Every companion below is available white-label in Europe today
(manufacturer notes at the end).

like wine), backed by the published COA. Grade names stay honest and geographic (Do'an /
Reserve), not invented numbers.

## Cross-house mechanics (the AOV machine)

1. **Per-serving price on every product card** (Reformed's discipline: "€1.30/day").
2. **Subscribe & Save 15%** on heroes and companions; subscription is a flexible box — swap
   SKUs each cycle, pause/skip/cancel. First subscription order ships with the accessory free.
3. **1/2/3-jar and 1/3/6-supply ladders** with MOST CHOSEN / BEST VALUE badges (Sunna's
   strongest mechanic, kept — the badge language stays calm, no scratch cards).
4. **Ritual kits** (the starter-AOV play, target €75–90 first order):
   - **The Morning Ritual** — Sidr sticks + collagen sachets + BSO softgels, 14 days. €59
   - **The Gift of Purity** — tasting flight + olive-wood spoon, gift-wrapped. €45
   - **Ramadan: Suhoor & Iftar box** — dates + sidr minis + electrolyte-free simple kit,
     seasonal, pre-orderable (Sunna's sold-out Suhoor SKU proved demand and fumbled supply).
5. **Free EU shipping at €55** — just above one hero jar, pulling a companion into cart.
6. **Loyalty**: auto-enrolled points (1pt/€1), redemptions as % off; birthday + Eid bonuses.
7. **Proof loop**: every pack carries a QR → batch page (COA, pollen report, TQ chromatogram,
   halal certificate with body + number + expiry). Proof is merchandising, not compliance.

## Launch sequencing

- **Wave 1 (launch):** Sidr hero jar + Sidr sticks + Marine collagen hero + sachets +
  Tasting flight. Two certifiable houses, five SKUs, one gift box.
- **Wave 2 (+3 months):** BSO hero + softgels + Black Seed Honey + Qahwa+Collagen + Reserve.
- **Wave 3 (seasonal):** Ramadan box (pre-order 6 weeks ahead), Karak+Collagen, TQ 3.0+.

## White-label / manufacturing notes (from the trend scan)

- Collagen sachets & powders, halal gummy/softgel lines: **Elit Biotech GmbH** (DE),
  **White Label Collagen**, **BF-ESSE** (EU), **A4 Group** (UK/EU), build-your-own-brand.com
  (halal private label programs).
- Honey formats need a **honey co-packer**, not a supplement CMO (cheaper, faster);
  stick-pack filling is the only specialised step.
- BSO softgels: standard EU CMOs; halal shell material per the QC spec (docs/03).
- Every supplier engagement runs on `docs/03-supplier-qc-specs.md` — the spec sheet is the
  moat that keeps "premium" true.
