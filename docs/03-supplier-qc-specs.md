# SAFA Nutrition — Supplier QC Specifications

**Purpose.** These are the exact specifications to send to suppliers and contract manufacturers.
Every clause exists to close a substitution loophole — the difference between "sidr honey" and
*Wadi Do'an autumn-harvest sidr with a pollen report* is the entire brand. Nothing ships, and no
invoice is paid in full, until the paperwork in each section is in hand and passes.

**How to use.** Copy the relevant SKU section into the RFQ / purchase contract verbatim.
The "Reject if" lines are contractual rejection criteria. The "Documents required per batch"
lists are conditions of payment, not requests.

**General terms (all SKUs)**

- Facility: GMP-certified; HACCP or ISO 22000 in place; BRC/IFS preferred. Certificate copies
  current, with expiry dates, before first PO.
- Halal: certificate from a recognised body (e.g. HQC, HFCE, Halal Correct in NL; JAKIM/MUI-recognised
  bodies acceptable), naming the certifying body, certificate number, scope (product + facility),
  and expiry. "Halal compliant" without a certificate is not accepted and the word will not
  appear on our packaging until this is on file.
- Per-batch COA from an ISO 17025-accredited third-party lab (not only the supplier's in-house lab).
  We reserve the right to re-test any batch at an EU lab of our choice; a failed re-test is a
  rejected batch at supplier's cost, including freight.
- Pre-shipment sample from the actual production batch (not a "typical" sample), batch-coded,
  shipped before bulk release. Retained samples: supplier keeps 2× retail units per batch for
  24 months.
- Traceability: batch code on every unit linking to raw-material lot, production date, and COA.
- Third-party pre-shipment inspection (SGS / Bureau Veritas / Intertek) at our option for
  orders above agreed volume.
- Shelf life: minimum 75% of stated shelf life remaining at delivery. Stability data supporting
  the stated shelf life available on request.
- EU compliance: product and labelling must be compliant for sale in the Netherlands/EU
  (Reg. 1169/2011 food information; Reg. 2023/915 contaminant limits; supplement SKUs per
  Directive 2002/46/EC; honey per Directive 2001/110/EC).

## SKU 1 — Wild Yemeni Sidr Honey (Signature)

**What we are buying.** Raw, monofloral Sidr (Ziziphus spina-christi) honey from Yemen —
Hadhramaut, Wadi Do'an preferred — autumn harvest, from named beekeeping cooperatives.
Not "sidr-style", not "sidr blend", not Kashmiri or Pakistani sidr sold as Yemeni.

### Identity & origin — the substitution locks
- Botanical origin: monofloral Ziziphus spina-christi confirmed by **melissopalynology
  (pollen analysis)** per batch — Ziziphus pollen dominant, consistent with monofloral sidr.
  Report from an accredited honey lab (e.g. QSI Bremen, Intertek Bremen) required.
- Geographic origin: Yemen, region named (governorate + wadi). Harvest season and year stated
  per batch. Beekeeper/cooperative identity documented (name or coded ID we can audit).
- Processing: raw. Never heated above 40 °C at any point (declaration required); coarse-strained
  only, never fine-filtered or pressure-filtered (pollen must remain — it is our proof);
  no creaming, no blending across harvests or regions without written approval.

### Per-batch analytical panel — reject if outside limits
| Parameter | Specification | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| Moisture | ≤ 19% | Fermentation risk; watered honey |
| HMF | ≤ 15 mg/kg at shipment | Freshness; proves no heating (EU limit is 40 — we buy far under it) |
| Diastase activity | ≥ 8 Schade units | Heat/age damage indicator |
| C4 sugar adulteration (AOAC 998.12, SCIRA) | ≤ 7% (i.e. none detectable) | Cane/corn syrup adulteration |
| C3/rice & beet syrup screen (NMR honey profiling, e.g. QSI) | Pass / no foreign syrup markers | Modern syrups invisible to C4 test |
| Foreign enzymes (e.g. beta/gamma-amylase) | Not detected | Syrup-conversion tell-tale |
| Electrical conductivity | Report value; consistent with sidr profile | Botanical consistency check |
| Sucrose | ≤ 5 g/100 g | Honey directive + feeding fraud |
| Antibiotic residues: chloramphenicol, nitrofurans, tetracyclines, sulfonamides, streptomycin | Not detected (LC-MS/MS panel) | The classic failure point of Yemeni/Gulf honey at EU customs — non-negotiable |
| Heavy metals: Pb | ≤ 0.10 mg/kg | Reg. 2023/915 honey limit |
| Pesticide screen (multi-residue) | Compliant with EU MRLs | Import compliance |
| Microbiology: yeast & mould | Report; consistent with raw honey ≤19% moisture | Stability |

- Sensory: colour dark amber to reddish; taste profile characteristic of sidr (caramel,
  mild bitterness on finish); no smoke taint, no fermentation nose. We taste every batch
  against the approved golden sample before release.
- **Reject if:** pollen report absent or non-monofloral; any adulteration marker positive;
  any antibiotic detected; HMF > 15; moisture > 19%; batch blended across regions; heated.

### Documents required per batch
1. Pollen analysis report (accredited lab)
2. Full COA covering the table above (accredited lab)
3. Origin declaration: governorate, wadi, cooperative/beekeeper ID, harvest month + year
4. Raw/never-heated + no-filtration declaration (signed)
5. Halal certificate of facility (honey itself is halal; the handling facility is certified)

### Packaging spec (if supplier fills)
- Glass jar only, food-grade lid with tamper-evident seal; fill weight 250 g ± 2%;
  batch code + harvest year laser-etched or printed on base/lid, not only on the label.

## SKU 2 — Halal Marine Collagen Peptides (Volume anchor)

**What we are buying.** Hydrolysed Type I marine collagen peptides from **wild-caught fish
skin/scales** — species named — unflavoured, no fillers, formulated (by us) with vitamin C,
zinc, biotin. Not bovine, not porcine, not "marine blend" with undeclared species, not cut
with maltodextrin.

### Identity & source — the substitution locks
- Raw material: fish skin and/or scales; **species declared** (e.g. cod/pollock or tilapia)
  and consistent between batches — species switching requires written approval (allergen
  labelling and halal file both depend on it). Country of raw-material origin and country of
  processing both declared.
- Wild-caught vs farmed declared; if farmed, antibiotic residue panel required.
- Process: enzymatic hydrolysis; solvent-free; no ionising radiation; no GMO processing aids.
- **Purity: 100% collagen peptides.** No maltodextrin, no silica flow agents, no sweeteners,
  no "proprietary blend". Protein content is the tell — see panel.

### Per-batch analytical panel — reject if outside limits
| Parameter | Specification | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| Protein (Kjeldahl, dry basis, N×5.55 collagen factor) | ≥ 90% | Filler/cutting detection — cheap powders run 70–80% |
| Hydroxyproline | ≥ 10 g/100 g protein | Confirms it is actually collagen, not whey/soy filler |
| Average molecular weight | ≤ 3,000 Da (target 2,000 Da), distribution report | Bioavailability claim substance; "collagen" at 30 kDa is gelatine |
| Moisture | ≤ 8% | Caking, microbial risk, weight padding |
| Ash | ≤ 2.5% | Scale-mineral residue / processing quality |
| pH (10% solution) | 5.0–7.0 | Process control |
| Solubility | 100% clear in cold water, no sediment, ≤ 30 s stir | Consumer experience; poor hydrolysis shows here |
| Odour/taste | Neutral; no fishy/TMA off-note in 10 g/250 ml water panel test | Deodorisation quality — the #1 cheap-collagen giveaway |
| Heavy metals: Pb / Cd / Hg / As | ≤ 3.0 / 1.0 / 0.10 / 1.5 mg/kg (supplement limits, Reg. 2023/915 + 2002/46 practice) | Marine raw material risk |
| Microbiology: TVC ≤ 10³ cfu/g; yeast+mould ≤ 10² cfu/g; Enterobacteriaceae absent/g; Salmonella absent/25 g; S. aureus absent/g | All pass | Food safety |
| Peroxide value (if any lipid fraction) | Report | Rancidity |

- **Reject if:** protein < 90%; hydroxyproline low (indicates blending); MW report absent;
  fishy odour in the cup test; any undeclared excipient found; species differs from declaration.

### Documents required per batch
1. COA covering the full panel (accredited lab)
2. Species + origin declaration; catch method (wild/farmed)
3. Halal certificate covering the **collagen production line** (processing aids, cross-contact) —
   fish is halal by default, the certificate is about the process; body, number, expiry on file
4. Allergen statement (fish — declared; facility cross-contact statement for gluten, milk, soy,
   crustaceans, egg)
5. GMP/ISO 22000/HACCP facility certificates (annual)
6. MW distribution chromatogram (can be per production campaign if process unchanged)

### Finished-product spec (when supplier co-packs the formulated powder)
- Formulation per our recipe only: collagen peptides + vitamin C (form specified, e.g. ascorbic
  acid), zinc (citrate/bisglycinate), biotin. No flavours/sweeteners in Unflavoured SKU.
- Homogeneity: active-nutrient assay per batch within 90–125% of label claim (EU tolerance
  guidance); scoop calibrated to stated dose.
- Fill: 300 g ± 2%; nitrogen flush or desiccant-free double-seal per our packaging spec;
  tamper-evident inner seal mandatory.

## SKU 3 — Cold-Pressed Black Seed Oil (Nigella sativa)

**What we are buying.** Virgin, cold-pressed, unrefined Nigella sativa seed oil — single
origin (Ethiopian or Egyptian seed, origin named), standardised thymoquinone content —
for bottled oil and (softgel SKU) halal-certified softgels. Not solvent-extracted, not
refined/deodorised oil "boosted" with synthetic TQ, not blended with cheaper carrier oils.

### Identity & source — the substitution locks
- Seed origin: country + region named per batch; single-origin per batch (no multi-origin
  blending without approval). Harvest year declared.
- Extraction: mechanical cold press only, seed temperature ≤ 40 °C during pressing
  (declaration required); **no solvent extraction anywhere in the line** (hexane residue test
  is the audit); unrefined — no bleaching, no deodorising, no winterising unless approved.
- No additives: no synthetic thymoquinone spiking (TQ level must be consistent with the
  natural fatty-acid profile), no antioxidant additives (TBHQ/BHT) unless approved, no
  dilution — fatty-acid profile is the fingerprint, see panel.

### Per-batch analytical panel — reject if outside limits
| Parameter | Specification | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| Thymoquinone (HPLC) | ≥ 2.0% (premium grade; 1.5% absolute floor) | The active — and the grade we're paying for |
| Free fatty acids (as oleic) | ≤ 2.5% | Seed quality + freshness |
| Peroxide value | ≤ 10 meq O₂/kg at shipment (target ≤ 5) | Rancidity — cold-pressed BSO fails here when old |
| p-Anisidine value | ≤ 15 | Secondary oxidation (PV alone can be gamed) |
| Fatty-acid profile (GC) | Linoleic 50–60%, oleic 18–25%, palmitic 10–14% — consistent with pure N. sativa | Dilution fingerprint: sunflower/soy cutting shifts this |
| Hexane residue | Not detected | Proves no solvent extraction |
| Heavy metals: Pb / Cd / Hg / As | ≤ 3.0 / 1.0 / 0.10 / 1.5 mg/kg | Supplement limits |
| Aflatoxins (B1, total) | B1 ≤ 2 µg/kg; total ≤ 4 µg/kg | Nigella seed mould risk |
| Pesticide multi-residue | EU MRL compliant | Import compliance |
| Microbiology (oil) | TVC ≤ 10³ cfu/g; pathogens absent | Safety |
| Sensory | Pungent, peppery, characteristic; no rancid/paint note | Freshness panel vs golden sample |

- **Reject if:** TQ < contracted grade; PV or AnV over limit; FA profile inconsistent with
  pure N. sativa; hexane detected; aflatoxin over limit; origin not as declared.

### Softgel SKU additional spec
- Shell gelatine: **halal-certified bovine gelatine (certificate naming slaughter compliance)
  or fish gelatine** — porcine is an immediate contract breach; shell-material halal
  certificate on file before first production.
- Fill: 1000 mg oil per softgel ± 5%; oil inside must meet the full panel above (same batch
  documentation); no mineral-oil lubricant residue on shells.
- Disintegration ≤ 30 min; softgel COA covers fill weight, disintegration, micro.

### Documents required per batch
1. COA covering the full panel (accredited lab), incl. TQ% by HPLC with chromatogram
2. Origin + harvest-year declaration; cold-press ≤ 40 °C declaration; solvent-free declaration
3. Halal certificate (pressing facility; softgel line + shell material for softgel SKU)
4. Allergen cross-contact statement (sesame/mustard commonly co-pressed — declare the line)
5. Filling: amber glass only for bottled SKU; nitrogen headspace; tamper-evident cap;
   batch + best-before printed on bottle, not only the label

## Negotiation posture (applies to all three)

1. **Golden sample first.** Approved reference sample locked before first PO; every batch is
   judged against it. "Equal to golden sample" is written into the contract.
2. **Paperwork = payment.** 30/70 or 50/50 split with the balance released only after COA +
   declarations for that batch are received and pass. Never 100% upfront.
3. **Name the labs.** For honey: QSI or Intertek Bremen. For collagen/BSO: any ISO 17025 lab,
   but we name the panel. Suppliers who push back on third-party testing are self-identifying.
4. **Batch size discipline.** First order small (pilot), full panel at their cost; volume
   pricing only after two consecutive clean batches.
5. **Publishing clause.** We publish batch COAs on the storefront — the supplier is told this
   up front. It deters quiet spec drift better than any audit.
