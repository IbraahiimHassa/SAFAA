The qahwa & the dose
Lightly roasted arabica ground fine for the dallah, with true hail cardamom and negin-grade saffron threads — the blend your grandmother would recognise — plus hydrolysed marine collagen that dissolves silently in the simmer.
How to brew it
Two scoops (15.2 g) per small dallah. Bring to the edge of a simmer — never a rolling boil — twice. Rest a minute, pour into small cups, serve with dates. One dallah carries the full daily dose.

What's actually in it

Ingredients, and what we can prove.

Claims here use EFSA-authorised wording only. Where a benefit isn't authorised, we don't imply it — we publish the number instead.

Sidr (Ziziphus)
The autumn flowering of the Sidr tree in Wadi Do'an. Pollen analysis on every batch is what proves the honey is monofloral — and what a blend can never show you.
Raw, unheated honey
Coarse-strained at the apiary, never pasteurised. HMF is stated per batch — the number that tells you whether honey has been heated or aged.
Marine collagen
Type I peptides hydrolysed from wild-caught fish skin to ~2 kDa, the size the absorption research used. Marine by design: the lowest-friction halal choice.
Nigella sativa
Ethiopian black seed, mechanically pressed below 40 °C. Thymoquinone measured by HPLC and printed on the front — the grade, not a range.
Vitamin C
Ascorbic acid at 80 mg — 100% RI. Carries the one authorised claim we are allowed to make: vitamin C contributes to normal collagen formation for the normal function of skin.
Zinc & biotin
Zinc citrate and D-biotin at authorised levels. Both contribute to the maintenance of normal hair, skin and nails — EFSA wording, nothing embroidered on top.
