The matcha & the dose
First-harvest, stone-ground ceremonial matcha from Japan, blended with hydrolysed marine collagen peptides. One scoop per bowl, 80 °C water, whisk. Drink it straight or as a latte — the collagen dissolves clear and changes nothing about the taste.

How to use it

Marine collagen
