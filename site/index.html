Named, like it matters.

Most "Yemeni Sidr" is a blend of countries and seasons sold on a story. Ours is one valley — Wadi Do'an in Hadhramaut — one autumn flowering of the Sidr tree, and beekeepers we can name. The pollen in the jar is the fingerprint that proves it: Ziziphus, dominant, batch after batch.

The same discipline runs the whole index. The collagen names its fish species and its molecular weight. The black seed names its seed origin and its thymoquinone number. If a figure can't be traced, it doesn't go on the label.

On the word halal: the seal and the word appear on our packaging only with a named certifying body, certificate number and expiry on file — published on the batch page. Until then, we show the paperwork we do have.

Questions

Asked, answered.

Is everything you sell halal?
Our range is built to be halal from the source up — marine (fish) collagen rather than bovine or porcine, honey and cold-pressed seed oil. We only print the word and the seal once a named certification body, certificate number and expiry date are on file, and we publish those documents on each product's batch page rather than asking you to take our word for it.
What exactly do you test for?
Honey: pollen origin, C4-sugar and syrup adulteration, antibiotic residues, HMF, diastase, moisture. Collagen: protein content, hydroxyproline, molecular weight, heavy metals, microbiology. Black seed oil: thymoquinone by HPLC, peroxide and acid values, fatty-acid fingerprint, solvent residues. Every batch, independent accredited lab, results published before sale.
How does the subscription work?
Subscribe & Save takes 15% off every delivery. You can swap products between deliveries, pause, skip or cancel at any time — the subscription is a standing order, not a lock-in.
Where do you deliver?
Across the EU. Delivery is free over €55; orders placed before 15:00 CET leave the Amsterdam warehouse the same working day.
